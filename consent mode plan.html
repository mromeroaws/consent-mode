Key technical advice

Five rules for the build
1Consent script→ 2HubSpot→ 3GTM / GA4 Required load order, so the first GA4 event carries the right consent state.

Keep region lists in sync

HubSpot decides which banner a visitor sees, but Google's script sets the default consent with its region parameter. The two country lists must be kept in sync by hand.

Guarantee load order

The consent script runs before HubSpot and GTM so the first GA4 event reflects the correct state.

Don't set gcs by hand

The Google tag adds this value automatically. The team only needs to confirm the consent state is right.

New editor: open questions Waiting on HubSpot

HubSpot still has to confirm whether banner types can differ by country, and how the listener behaves when a visitor rejects.

Don't migrate yet. Before pressing Switch to the new editor, take a screenshot of the current settings and wait for HubSpot to confirm the migration can be reversed.

Questions for AWS Legal

Every vendor defers the final approach to Legal. These answers decide the region list, the defaults and the banner platform.

Which model applies in each region

  1. Which countries or regions require opt-in? Confirm the final list, including the EEA, UK and Switzerland 🇪🇺🇬🇧🇨🇭. HubSpot's privacy settings also list Canada and Brazil 🇨🇦🇧🇷: do they apply to AWS?
  2. In the US 🇺🇸, does any state law that applies to AWS require an opt-out, a "Do Not Sell or Share" link, or honoring Global Privacy Control (GPC)?HubSpot's banner is confirmed to target by country only, not by US state.
  3. Outside opt-in regions, is tracking on by default acceptable, with a notice and an option to decline?

Advanced Mode before consent

  1. Advanced Mode sends cookieless signals to Google before a visitor in an opt-in region answers the banner. Is that acceptable there?
  2. What should happen for visitors who never interact with the banner, in each region?

What "Reject" should mean

  1. When a visitor rejects, should all analytics tracking stop? Should existing Google Analytics cookies be deleted or just stop updating?
  2. Which categories (analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization) should be denied by default in each region?If AWS doesn't use Google Ads, denying the ad categories everywhere may be simplest.

Other tools on the site

  1. Which other scripts or vendors need consent before they run?

Text, records and policies

  1. Define the banner text for each region, and update the Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy to match the final setup.
  2. Decide whether visitors' consent choices must be recorded, and where.
  3. Decide whether the legal requirements favor HubSpot's banner or a dedicated platform such as CookieYes.If Legal needs targeting by US state or GPC support, that could decide the platform.
